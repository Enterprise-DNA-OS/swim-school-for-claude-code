# Safety and policy evidence

Checked 2026-09-30. These checks identify missing or stale records. They do not verify a person with a regulator, decide exemption eligibility or certify that a lesson is safe. Staff must establish the rules that apply to each location and role, and respond to live regulator notices. Other Australian states are not covered by the NSW rule.

| Rule | Scope and implemented check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| NSW_WWCC | NSW instructor records: flag missing verification date/reference, unknown or barred status, and missing/expired clearance dates. Application status requires operator review. | [NSW Office of the Children's Guardian: register and verify](https://ocg.nsw.gov.au/working-children-check/wwcc-information-organisations/help-register-and-verify) |
| NZ_SAFETY | NZ instructors explicitly marked nz_required: flag missing full-check reference, missing date, non-cleared status or three years elapsed. A private swim school is not automatically in statutory scope. | [Oranga Tamariki: safety checking](https://www.orangatamariki.govt.nz/working-with-children/childrens-act-requirements/safety-checking/) |
| POLICY_FIRST_AID | Missing or expired first-aid evidence. This is the demo school's policy reminder, not a universal legal period. | School policy, replace with the operator's documented requirements. |
| POLICY_CONTACT | Missing emergency contact or consent reference. This is a school record policy. | School policy, confirm the actual consent and privacy requirements. |
| POLICY_CAPACITY | Active class count exceeds the smaller of class capacity and level maximum. No statutory swimmer ratio is asserted. | School staffing and pool risk assessment, configured by the operator. |

NSW employers verify through the official online service and keep evidence of each verification. A date in this database cannot detect a later bar; official employer notifications remain essential. Do not copy identity documents into notes.

The NZ Children's Act requirements apply to specified children's workers in government-funded organisations. A complete safety check involves more than police vetting. Keep the full check evidence externally and store its reference, with the date and responsible operator review. The program's three-year reminder runs only after explicit applicability configuration.

Swimming ratios depend on programme, pool, age, skill and supervision arrangements. max_students is a school policy setting. Skill progression requires instructor judgement. No automated record check replaces supervision, rescue capability or incident response.
